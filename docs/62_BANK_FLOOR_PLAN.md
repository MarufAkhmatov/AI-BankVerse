# AI BankVerse — Single-Floor Floor Plan

## 1. Absolute Rule

ONE FLOOR.

The entire floor is one continuous architectural environment.

---

# 3. Side Perimeter

Long side walls contain:

- windows
- columns
- arches
- banker stations
- private rooms

---

# 4. Central Axis

The central axis contains:

```
ENTRANCE

↓

RECEPTION

↓

OPEN CUSTOMER AREA

↓

CENTRAL EMBLEM

↓

REAR ARCHITECTURE
```

---

# 5. Navigation

The player must be able to walk:

Entrance → Reception

Reception → Any Department

Department → Department

Department → Private Banking

Private Banking → Main Hall

---

# 6. No Forced Corridors

The hall must remain visually open.

Do not create a maze.

---

# 7. NPC Navigation

NPC navigation paths must use:

- perimeter paths
- central paths
- department paths
- reception paths

---

# 8. Camera

The main cinematic composition is aligned with the
central longitudinal axis.

This creates strong symmetry.

---

# 9. Architectural Perspective

The player should be able to see a significant portion
of the bank from almost any major position.

This reinforces the feeling of one enormous hall.
