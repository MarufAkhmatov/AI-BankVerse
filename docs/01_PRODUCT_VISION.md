# AI BankVerse — Product Vision

## 1. Product Definition

AI BankVerse is a next-generation immersive digital banking platform where banking services are represented as a living 3D virtual bank.

The user does not primarily navigate traditional mobile banking menus.

Instead, the user enters a virtual bank environment and interacts with:

- AI bank employees
- AI receptionists
- AI financial advisors
- AI service specialists
- virtual banking facilities
- contextual service interfaces
- voice-driven banking workflows

The product combines:

1. 3D banking environment
2. AI agents
3. Voice interaction
4. Traditional banking functionality
5. Spatial navigation
6. Conversational UI
7. Context-aware service execution

---

## 2. Core Product Philosophy

The system must follow this principle:

> IMMERSION IS OPTIONAL. EFFICIENCY IS MANDATORY.

Users may explore the virtual bank manually.

However, users must never be forced to physically walk through the bank to complete a simple operation.

Example:

User:

"Elektr energiyasi uchun to'lov qilmoqchiman."

The reception AI should immediately understand the request and open the electricity payment interface.

The user does not need to walk to the payments department.

---

## 3. Product Experience

When the application starts:

1. User enters the virtual bank.
2. Reception AI welcomes the user.
3. The bank environment becomes visible.
4. Multiple departments exist around the user.
5. AI employees occupy physical positions.
6. Other virtual customers move through the environment.
7. User can walk around.
8. User can speak naturally.
9. AI agents understand intent.
10. Appropriate service interface opens.

---

- Business Banking

---

## 5. Two Interaction Modes

### Mode A — Immersive

User physically walks through the virtual bank.

Example:

Reception
→ Credit Department
→ Credit Agent
→ Conversation
→ Application

### Mode B — Fast Service

User talks to reception.

Example:

"Men kredit olmoqchiman."

Reception:

"Albatta. Siz uchun kredit variantlarini ochaman."

Credit interface appears immediately.

---

## 6. Product Differentiation

AI BankVerse must not feel like:

- a traditional banking app
- a chatbot
- a simple 3D menu
- a game with banking buttons

It must feel like:

> A living digital bank powered by AI.

---

## 7. Visual Target

Visual quality must NOT resemble:

- 1990s games
- early 2000s games
- low-poly games
- old mobile games
- Avatar: The Game graphics copied literally

Visual target:

- modern AAA 3D
- realistic materials
- physically based rendering
- cinematic lighting
- realistic human characters
- realistic animation
- high-quality environments
- dynamic shadows
- realistic reflections
- natural facial animation
- modern third-person camera

Reference era:

2020s AAA game production quality.

---

## 8. Intellectual Property Rule

Avatar: The Game is used only as a high-level visual inspiration for:

- third-person perspective
- immersive exploration
- environmental scale
- cinematic feeling

Do NOT copy:

- Avatar characters
- Na'vi
- Pandora
- Avatar logos
- Avatar locations
- Avatar assets
- Avatar animations
- Avatar sounds
- Avatar UI
- Avatar story

AI BankVerse must have its own original visual identity.

---

## 9. Success Definition

The user should open the application and think:

"I am inside a bank."

Not:

"I am looking at a banking application."

This is the central product objective.
