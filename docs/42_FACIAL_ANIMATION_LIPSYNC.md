# AI BankVerse — Facial Animation & Lip Sync

## 1. Objective

Conversation must look believable.

---

# 2. Facial States

Neutral

Listening

Thinking

Happy

Professional

Concerned

Confused

Confirming

---

# 3. Lip Sync

```
Audio:

TTS output

↓

phoneme/viseme analysis

↓

facial animation
```

---

# 4. Eye Behavior

Eyes should:

look at player

look at screen

look away naturally

blink

change focus

---

# 5. Head Behavior

Use subtle:

nod

turn

tilt

---

# 6. Listening

When user speaks:

Agent should:

look at user

occasionally nod

maintain natural attention

---

# 7. Speaking

While speaking:

mouth moves

eyes remain engaged

occasional gestures

---

# 8. Thinking

Avoid frozen face.

Use:

subtle eye movement

short gaze shift

micro-expression

---

# 9. Emotion

Emotion is controlled by AI response metadata.

Example:

emotion = professional_positive

But emotion must not modify financial logic.

---

# 10. Performance

Close characters:

full facial quality

Distant characters:

reduced facial update rate
