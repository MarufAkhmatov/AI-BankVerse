# AI BankVerse — Flagship Bank Architecture

## 1. Purpose

This document is the authoritative architectural brief for the AI BankVerse flagship branch.
It supersedes the two-floor sketch in docs/06 and docs/43.

---

# 2. Non-Negotiable: One Floor

The bank is strictly one floor. No second floor, no mezzanine, no upper banking level, no
multi-story customer circulation. See docs/70 for the full rationale.

---

# 3. Architectural Scale

45–60 meters long

25–35 meters wide

14–18 meters ceiling height

---

# 4. Architectural Style

An original architectural language inspired by historic monumental banking halls —
Beaux-Arts, Renaissance, and Neo-Gothic detailing, European private banking, early
20th-century Grand-Central-scale interiors — combined with cinematic, James Bond-level
sophistication. Do not copy any real building. See docs/63_VISUAL_REFERENCE_BIBLE.md
for what is and is not taken from reference imagery.

---

# 5. Central Composition

```
Entrance
→ Reception
→ Open central customer area
→ Banker stations
→ Private banking perimeter
→ Rear monumental architecture
```

Everything remains on one floor.

---

# 6. Materials

Marble, dark walnut, antique brass, aged bronze, crystal, velvet.

---

# 7. Lighting

7–9 chandeliers, large windows admitting natural daylight, warm artificial accent lighting.

---

# 8. Ceiling

Vaulted, ornamental, 14–18 meters — see docs/33_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §4.

---

# 9. Columns and Arches

Large columns and repeated arches frame the hall's central axis and perimeter.

---

# 10. Windows

Large vertical windows along the long side walls, per docs/62_BANK_FLOOR_PLAN.md §3.

---

# 11. Banker Stations

16–24 stations, distributed along the central hall. See §11 below (original text) for
per-station behavior requirements.

---

# 12. Central Customer Area

The open area between reception and the banker stations is the primary circulation space —
see docs/62_BANK_FLOOR_PLAN.md for the full floor plan.

---

# 13. Central Hall

It must allow:

- free walking
- customer circulation
- cinematic camera movement
- NPC navigation

Do not overcrowd the hall.

---

# 14. Reception

Reception is positioned immediately inside the entrance.

It must be visually dominant but not oversized.

Materials:

dark walnut

cream marble

antique bronze

subtle gold

---

# 15. Private Banking

Private banking rooms are positioned along the perimeter
of the SAME FLOOR.

Use:

bronze-framed glass

dark walnut

emerald leather

warm lighting

These rooms must remain visibly connected to the main hall.

---

# 16. VIP Lounge

One VIP lounge exists on the same floor.

It contains:

- lounge furniture
- bookshelves
- marble
- brass
- emerald leather
- dark walnut
- private consultation room

---

# 17. Technology

Technology is embedded into architecture.

Avoid obvious sci-fi interfaces.

Examples:

marble-integrated displays

bronze-framed transparent screens

interactive banking desks

subtle glass interfaces

---

# 18. Visual Philosophy

The architectural message is:

"2026 technology hidden inside a 100-year-old financial palace."

---

# 19. Final Visual Test

A screenshot from the entrance must communicate:

FINANCIAL CATHEDRAL

before the user sees any UI.
