# AI BankVerse — Character Asset Pipeline

## 1. Character Philosophy

Characters are a major part of the product identity.

They must look like modern digital humans.

They must NOT resemble:

- retro games
- low-poly NPCs
- cartoon banking mascots
- generic primitive mannequins

---

# 2. Character Categories

---

# 3. Employee Design

Every employee needs:

- unique face
- hairstyle
- clothing
- age range
- body type
- voice
- personality
- department identity

---

# 4. Banking Uniformity

Characters should visually belong to the same institution.

Use consistent:

- corporate colors
- clothing language
- ID badge
- accessories

Avoid making every character look identical.

---

# 5. Player Character

Player customization:

- gender
- hairstyle
- clothing
- skin tone
- body proportions
- accessories

Optional:

- name
- voice

---

# 6. AI Agent Identity

Every AI employee must have a visible identity.

Example:

AZIZA

Credit Specialist

● Available

---

# 7. Facial Animation

Required:

- blinking
- eye tracking
- lip sync
- eyebrows
- mouth movement
- subtle expressions

---

# 8. Character LOD

LOD0:

close interaction

LOD1:

normal interaction distance

LOD2:

medium distance

LOD3:

background

LOD4:

very distant

---

# 9. Background NPC Optimization

Background NPCs may use:

- simplified skeletal meshes
- reduced animation frequency
- animation sharing
- pooled actors

# 12. Quality Requirement

Close-up conversations must withstand cinematic camera distance.

No:

- obvious texture stretching
- broken facial animation
- unnatural eyes
- stiff hands
- robotic movement
