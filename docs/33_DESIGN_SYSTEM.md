# AI BankVerse — Visual Design System

## 1. Visual Philosophy

2020s AAA production quality, expressed through material richness and light rather than
raw polygon count. See docs/63_VISUAL_REFERENCE_BIBLE.md for the full reference language.

---

# 2. Color Palette

Warm neutrals — cream marble, dark walnut — accented with antique bronze and brass.
Emerald leather is the single saturated accent color, reserved for private-banking areas.

---

# 3. Materials

Marble, dark walnut, antique bronze, brass, glass, leather. Every major material is PBR
with micro-surface detail — see docs/69_ASSET_PIPELINE.md §9 Quality Gate.

---

# 4. Lighting

Cinematic, warm, physically believable. Natural daylight through tall windows combined
with artificial chandelier light.

Realistic shadowing

---

# 5. Character Clothing

Professional banking attire.

Examples:

formal suit

business casual

bank uniform

premium advisor attire

---

# 6. UI

UI should visually integrate with the physical environment.

Avoid generic mobile-app card overload.

---

# 7. Motion

Everything should have subtle motion.

Examples:

screens

doors

NPCs

lighting

environment

characters

---

# 8. Camera

Third-person cinematic camera.

Default:

slightly behind and above shoulder.

---

# 9. Visual Quality Gate

Any asset that looks like:

PS2

PS3

early mobile 3D

low-poly prototype

must NOT be considered final quality.

Placeholder assets are allowed during development,

but must be explicitly tagged:
