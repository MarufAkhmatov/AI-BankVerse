# AI BankVerse — Audio & Environmental Sound

## 1. Objective

Sound is essential for immersion.

The player should feel that the bank is physically around them.

---

# 2. Ambient Sounds

Examples:

- footsteps
- conversations
- keyboard typing
- printer
- doors
- elevator
- air conditioning
- ATM sounds
- payment terminal
- distant announcements

---

# 3. Spatial Audio

Use 3D positional audio.

If an employee speaks behind the player:

voice should originate from behind.

---

# 4. Department Audio

Credit:

quiet professional environment

Payments:

higher activity

Reception:

moderate conversations

Waiting Area:

ambient conversations

---

# 5. Voice Priority

Current conversation has highest priority.

Background conversations must reduce volume dynamically.

---

# 6. Audio Zones

Each department has an audio zone.

Crossing a department boundary should smoothly change ambience.

---

# 7. Voice Ducking

When AI speaks:

ambient audio is reduced.

When AI stops:

ambient audio returns.

---

# 8. Sound Design Rule

Never make the environment completely silent.

The bank should feel operational.
