# AI BankVerse — Bank Manager Gameplay

## 1. Player Role

The player is a bank employee / manager.

The player can physically walk through the bank
and manage AI employees.

---

# 2. Manager Commands

Examples:

"To'lov bo'limidagi navbatni ko'r."

"Eng band agentlarni ko'rsat."

"Azizani kredit bo'limiga yubor."

"Bugungi xizmatlar bo'yicha hisobot ber."

"To'lov bo'limiga yordam kerakmi?"
