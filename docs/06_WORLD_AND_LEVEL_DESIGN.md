# AI BankVerse — World & Level Design

## 1. Main Environment

The first playable environment is a large modern bank branch.

Architecture:

```
GROUND FLOOR
├── Entrance
├── Reception
├── Waiting Area
├── Retail Banking
├── Payments
├── Cards
└── ATMs

SECOND FLOOR
├── Credit
├── Mortgage
├── Deposits
├── Premium Banking
└── Management

BACK OFFICE
├── Operations
├── AI Operations Center
├── Server / Infrastructure Visualization
└── Staff Area
```

---

## 2. Visual Style

Modern realistic banking architecture.

Materials:

- glass
- stone
- wood
- metal
- fabric
- polished surfaces

Lighting:

- daylight
- artificial ceiling lighting
- local accent lighting
- realistic reflections

---

## 3. Living Environment

The bank must never look empty.

NPC activities:

- walking
- sitting
- waiting
- talking
- typing
- reading
- checking phones
- entering/exiting

---

## 4. Dynamic Systems

The environment reacts to activity.

Examples:

High queue:

more customers appear

High workload:

agent becomes visibly busy

Completed transaction:

customer receives confirmation and leaves

Opening hours:

lighting/environment changes

---

## 5. Interactive Objects

Player can interact with:

- doors
- desks
- computers
- screens
- elevators
- chairs
- kiosks
- ATMs
- information displays

---

## 6. Third-Person Camera

Target:

modern third-person action-adventure camera.

Features:

- smooth follow
- shoulder offset
- camera collision
- indoor compression
- dynamic zoom
- interaction focus

---

## 7. No Combat

The world is not a combat environment.

All interaction is:

social
financial
operational
exploratory
